# Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is my favorite series — a *science fiction horror drama* set in the **1980s** that follows a group of kids uncovering supernatural mysteries in the small town of *Hawkins, Indiana.*

---

## Plot Overview
> “Friends don’t lie.” — *Eleven*

When **Will Byers** mysteriously disappears, his friends begin a desperate search. They meet **Eleven**, a girl with telekinetic powers who escaped from a secret lab. Together they uncover dark secrets about an alternate dimension called the **Upside Down**.

---

### Main Characters
- **Eleven (Millie Bobby Brown)** — A girl with psychic abilities  
- **Mike Wheeler (Finn Wolfhard)** — The determined leader of the group  
- **Dustin Henderson (Gaten Matarazzo)** — The witty, lovable nerd  
- **Lucas Sinclair (Caleb McLaughlin)** — Loyal and pragmatic friend  
- **Will Byers (Noah Schnapp)** — The boy whose disappearance sparks the story

---

### Why I Love It
1. The **mystery** and suspense keep every episode intense.  
2. The **1980s nostalgia** (soundtrack, pop culture, visuals) adds charm.  
3. The **friendship and character growth** are emotional and relatable.

---

### Fun Fact
`Stranger Things` was originally developed under the title **Montauk**, inspired by local conspiracy lore.
